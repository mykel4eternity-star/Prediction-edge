How to find a real edge

01 · Build your probability
Use recent xG, opponent strength, home/away splits, injuries and tactical matchups. Don't guess just to make a bet look valuable.
02 · Compare the whole market
For 1X2, compare all three outcomes and remove the bookmaker's overround to estimate fair market probabilities.
03 · Track calibration
Record estimates and actual outcomes. If 60% picks win far less often over a large sample, your model may be overconfident.
04 · Protect your bankroll
Positive expected value does not prevent losing streaks. Set a budget and never chase losses or stake essential money.
